# v0.6 Authority and Scientific Boundaries

## Purpose and result type

CPCF v0.6 is a closed-document, evidence-control system. It can validate, preserve, analyze, and
project declared records. Its native summary is an `operational_organization_profile` with thirteen
independent dimensions:

1. provenance integrity;
2. trust quorum;
3. temporal integrity;
4. structural reachability;
5. causal formation;
6. dimensional consistency;
7. exact self-maintenance;
8. finite-horizon resource persistence;
9. target-bound generative catalysis;
10. verification capacity;
11. effective independence;
12. coordination protocol integrity; and
13. perturbation robustness.

Each dimension is `satisfied`, `violated`, `unknown`, or `unknown_due_to_budget`. Compatibility is
true only when every contract-required dimension is satisfied in one immutable snapshot. The
profile is not a scalar intelligence measurement and does not make a phase, consciousness, or
superintelligence claim.

## Authority model

- Every native document has an API version, a closed kind, typed metadata and specification, and
  non-authoritative reverse-DNS extensions. Schema identity is part of what is verified.
- A DSSE payload binds the schema identity, subject digest, tenant, workspace, principal, role,
  scope, signing time, policy sequence, and trusted-time receipt. Envelope key identifiers are
  lookup hints, not authority by themselves.
- Verify authority using admitted public keys, protected payload, pinned identity, policy sequence,
  trusted time, and the applicable role quorum. Recompute cached validation values.
- For high-impact decisions, required signatures cover identical subjects and come from
  role-separated principals and keys. This is not threshold cryptography.
- Historical status distinguishes signing-time validity, subsequent expiry, prospective revocation,
  and retroactive compromise. Unsigned or local time cannot establish authoritative ordering.

## Source-of-record separation

CPCF's content-addressed store and SQLite index are authoritative for CPCF objects, projections,
closure, seeds, barriers, and planner results. External reports remain immutable source material;
their source system retains authority for its own decisions. In particular, CPCF can inspect or
project CCR mission/lease records, PIC finite checks and certificates, VEK verifier ecology, FOST
claims and obligations, and other registered handoffs. CPCF does not silently replace their
acceptance or authorization semantics.

## Non-claims

The mathematical tools are bounded formal accounting methods. Preserve these limits:

- Reachability is not causal formation.
- Exact stoichiometric balance or a finite resource trajectory is not a kinetic, stability,
  entropy-production, equilibrium, thermodynamic, or physical-feasibility result.
- A catalytic or organizational witness is not proof that a software collective is a chemical or
  physical phase.
- Independence requires admitted exposure evidence; labels, roles, principal names, model names,
  or missing records do not prove it.
- Perturbation results are confined to the declared reduced snapshots and their implementation
  bounds. Exhausted budgets and unmatched selectors remain unknown.
- Bounded strong planning does not add fairness, eventual-success, or general controllability.
  Hypothetical planner outputs do not become evidence until receipt-backed promotion.
- Trial binding checks compatibility of registered records; it does not certify causal
  identification or statistical analysis.

## Review sequence

1. Identify the immutable snapshot and analysis-basis digest.
2. Determine the source-of-record owner for every material external object.
3. Verify canonical document/schema identity and source pointer reconstruction.
4. Recompute DSSE/trust/time/quorum status from admitted materials.
5. Report the independent profile dimensions without collapsing them into a favorable overall
   story.
6. Identify missing evidence, budget limits, incomplete implementation rows, and the next
   least-effectful action.
