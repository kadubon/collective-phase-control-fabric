# Epistemic control and checked composition

Use `--epistemic` consistently to bind the fixed-model observation sidecar. Without
it, released rectangular-adversarial behavior remains in force. Replay this mode
with visible observation symbols, never hidden successor IDs. Timing, availability
and debug fields are excluded observation channels unless explicitly encoded.

Retain correlated pairs of fixed parameter and complete ledger state. Multiple
states for one parameter cannot be reduced to one favorable state or coordinate
bounds. A usable action and entry/continuation policy must work in all compatible
states. Unknown or inconsistent support is not success. A new kernel invalidates
old witnesses; an entry replay also retains its comparison checkpoint.

Information-use value keeps the paid probe and masks only its signal. Net sensing
benefit excludes the probe. Reoptimize both, match all other assumptions, and
report typed differences only after complete search. Neither support size nor
number of workflow names is an intelligence measure.

Synthesis builds bounded typed observation trees over immutable primitives.
Independently unfold every prefix and adverse observation. A finite-domain
certificate cannot authorize a composite or prove arbitrary program semantics.
Flatten macro steps; preserve time, queues, debt, reservations and activation depth.
Formation and per-use allowances remain distinct and charged. Failed synthesis
does not refund its declared costs. Cumulative enumeration work reserves bounded
allowances, not self-reported consumption.

Catalogue revisions are unsigned copy-on-write proposals. `--model-only` enables
only hypothetical reuse. Fresh original admission is required for evidence-driven
replanning; never substitute a cached assessment or promote fixture keys. Export
primitive job proposals with existing policy coverage and external authorization.

Admitted symbols and ledger records must fit one joint fixed-model receipt history.
Separate compatibility checks are insufficient. Use hidden ledger data only for
model-consistency validation; do not let it select a controller action or shrink
the public support outside the registered observation channel.
